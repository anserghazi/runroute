How it works

Three steps from your address to a route you can run.

  1. Set your start

    Type your address, or let RunRoute use your current location. Your start is also your finish — every route is a loop.

  2. Pick your places

    Choose a search radius and tap the nearby parks, sights and cafés you'd like your run to pass.

  3. Run the loop

    Get a door-to-door route with distance and time. Drag it to fine-tune, export a GPX, or share the link.
